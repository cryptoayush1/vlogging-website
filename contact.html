Editorial Directory

Write to the
journal.

Field Notes is an independent publication dedicated to thoughtful visual essays, honest craft, and ideas worth staying with.

We do not use contact forms, automated survey funnels, or subscription paywalls. Every note sent to our editorial desks is received and read directly by human editors.

“Good work asks better questions. If you have an observation, an essay outline, or a quiet note from the field, our inboxes are open.”
Editorial Review Cadence

We review story pitches, finished video essay drafts, and letters on a weekly cycle. Please allow two to three business days for a considered response from our editors.

01 / Submissions & Pitches

Story proposals & films

For essayists, documentarians, and visual researchers. We welcome video essay outlines, scripts, and finished film treatments. Please include a short synopsis, word count (under 1,500 words for essays), and any private preview links (Vimeo / YouTube unlisted).
